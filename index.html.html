Pet-friendly cleaning

Have pets? That's okay.

We welcome homes with pets. Please tell us during booking so we can prepare appropriately and select suitable cleaning products and methods.

Normal pet hair and routine cleaning in a pet household are included in regular cleaning. Additional charges may apply for extensive pet hair, urine stains, feces, strong odors, or other pet-related cleaning that requires significant additional time or specialized treatment.

For everyone's safety, pets should be secured in a safe area while our team is working.

We do not currently provide specialized biohazard or severe animal-waste remediation.
